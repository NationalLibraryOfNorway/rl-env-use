When performing multi-step numerical calculations:
- Carry full floating-point precision through every intermediate step. Do NOT round any intermediate result.
- Apply rounding only to the single final answer, at the precision requested.
- Example (invented): if gross profit = 7102 and divisor = 8188, compute 7102/8188 = 0.867367... then multiply or divide further before rounding — never write 0.8674 mid-calculation and continue from there.
- Off-by-one errors in the last decimal place almost always come from rounding an intermediate value too early.
